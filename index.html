About me

My work combines hands-on systems engineering with questions about trust, authorization, and reliability in AI-assisted security workflows. At Emphere, I build secure software supply-chain infrastructure and investigate how autonomous agents behave when asked to assess vulnerabilities. Earlier, at UMass Amherst, I worked on AI-powered vulnerability analysis and supported hands-on security education as a teaching assistant.

Emphere · Seattle, WA
